# XCUIDevice.Button.action (Swift)

**Framework:** XCUIAutomation  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst · watchOS · Xcode 16.3+

The device’s action button.

## Declaration

```swift
case action
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

The button you use for specific actions in workout apps, available in watchOS 9 and later on Apple Watch Ultra. You can use [hasHardwareButton(\_:)](../hashardwarebutton%28__%29.md) to check for its availability from your test.
