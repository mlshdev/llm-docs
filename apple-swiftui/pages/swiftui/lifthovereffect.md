# LiftHoverEffect

**Framework:** SwiftUI  
**Kind:** Structure  
**Availability:** iOS 18.0+ · iPadOS 18.0+ · Mac Catalyst 18.0+ · tvOS 18.0+ · visionOS 2.0+

A hover effect that slides the pointer under the view and disappears as the view scales up and gains a shadow.
