**Framework:** SwiftUI  
**Kind:** Type Property  
**Availability:** iOS 14.0+ · iPadOS 14.0+ · Mac Catalyst 14.0+ · macOS 11.0+ · visionOS 1.0+

## Discussion

By default, this group includes the following commands in macOS:

- Show/Hide Sidebar
- Enter/Exit Full Screen
