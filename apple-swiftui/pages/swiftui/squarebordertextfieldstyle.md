# SquareBorderTextFieldStyle

**Framework:** SwiftUI  
**Kind:** Structure  
**Availability:** macOS 10.15+ (deprecated in 27.2)

A text field style with a system-defined square border.

> Use BorderedTextField instead. As of macOS 26, text fields no longer have a rectangular border.
