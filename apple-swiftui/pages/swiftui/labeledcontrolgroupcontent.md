# LabeledControlGroupContent

**Framework:** SwiftUI  
**Kind:** Structure  
**Availability:** iOS 16.0+ · iPadOS 16.0+ · Mac Catalyst 16.0+ · macOS 13.0+ · tvOS 17.0+ · visionOS 1.0+

A view that represents the body of a control group with a specified label.

## Declaration
