# MenuControlGroupStyle

**Framework:** SwiftUI  
**Kind:** Structure  
**Availability:** iOS 16.4+ · iPadOS 16.4+ · Mac Catalyst 16.4+ · macOS 13.3+ · tvOS 17.0+ · visionOS 1.0+
