# Plans

> \[!TIP]
> Interested in pricing details? Check out the <a id="dkr_docs_index_pricing"></a>pricing page to compare plans.

To subscribe to a plan, see [Manage plans](https://docs.docker.com/subscription/manage/) or [Docker plans](https://docs.docker.com/subscription/plans/docker/). Plans can be tied to personal or organization account types, and include options to extend usage limits.

This section covers usage entitlements, billing cycle, and plan management options for each available plan.

To manage your plans by adding a new plan or upgrading an active plan, see [Manage plans](https://docs.docker.com/subscription/manage/).

## Product catalog

- [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/subscription/plans/docker/): Personal and organization plans, including build and runtime minutes.
- [Gordon plans](https://docs.docker.com/subscription/plans/gordon/): Usage plans that increase your Gordon allowance.
- [Docker Hardened Images (DHI)](https://docs.docker.com/subscription/plans/dhi/): Hardened image repositories for organization accounts.
- [AI Governance](https://docs.docker.com/subscription/plans/ai-governance/): Licenses for organization-wide AI policy enforcement.
- [Docker Verified Publisher (DVP)](https://docs.docker.com/subscription/plans/docker-verified-publisher/): Publisher analytics and reporting plans for organization accounts.
