# Manage your organization

As an organization owner, you manage your organization's membership, access,
and product usage. You can invite members, group them into teams, assign or
revoke licenses and seats, and change access to Docker products.

## Managing your organization

You manage your organization from [Docker Home](https://app.docker.com) and
must be assigned the
[organization owner role](https://docs.docker.com/enterprise/security/roles-and-permissions/).

## Seats and licenses

Seats and licenses both control access, but they apply to different kinds of
plans. The following table summarizes the difference.

| Entitlement | What it grants                                          | Applies to                                       | Managed from |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------ |
| Seat        | Membership in your Docker Team or Business subscription | Docker Core subscription                         | Billing      |
| License     | Access to a specific product or add-on                  | AI Governance, Docker Offload, and other add-ons | Members      |

For details, see [Seats](https://docs.docker.com/admin/organization/manage/manage-seats/)
and [License assignment](https://docs.docker.com/admin/organization/manage/manage-licenses/).

## Next steps

Explore the following sections to manage your organization.

- [Members](https://docs.docker.com/admin/organization/manage/members/): Invite, manage, and assign roles to your organization members.
- [Product access and usage](https://docs.docker.com/admin/organization/manage/manage-products/): Manage access and view usage for Docker products across your organization.
- [Security](https://docs.docker.com/enterprise/security/): Configure single sign-on, provisioning, and access management.
- [Billing](https://docs.docker.com/billing/): Manage payment methods and view billing history.
