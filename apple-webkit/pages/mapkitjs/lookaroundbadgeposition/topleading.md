# TopLeading

**Interface language:** Data

**Framework:** MapKit JS  
**Kind:** Enumeration Case  
**Availability:** MapKit JS 5.79+

Places the badge on the top leading corner of the Look Around preview.

```
readonly TopLeading: "topLeading";
```
