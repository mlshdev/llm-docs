# Drilldown

The Grafana Drilldown apps are designed for effortless data exploration through intuitive, queryless interactions.

Easily explore telemetry signals with these specialized tools, tailored specifically for the Grafana databases to provide quick and accurate insights.

> **Note**
>
> The Grafana Explore apps have changed to Grafana Drilldown apps.
> For example, Explore Logs is now Logs Drilldown.
> To learn more, read [Grafana Drilldown apps: the improved queryless experience formerly known as the Explore apps](https://grafana.com/blog/2025/02/20/grafana-drilldown-apps-the-improved-queryless-experience-formerly-known-as-the-explore-apps/).

To learn more, read:

- [From multi-line queries to no-code investigations: meeting Grafana users where they are](https://grafana.com/blog/2024/10/22/from-multi-line-queries-to-no-code-investigations-meeting-grafana-users-where-they-are/)
- [A queryless experience for exploring metrics, logs, traces, and profiles: Introducing the Drilldown apps suite for Grafana](https://grafana.com/blog/2024/09/24/queryless-metrics-logs-traces-profiles/).

[Watch the video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSHeWWsHaIA)

- [Grafana Metrics Drilldown](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/v13.2/visualizations/simplified-exploration/metrics/): Quickly find related metrics with a few clicks, without needing to write PromQL queries to retrieve metrics.
- [Grafana Logs Drilldown](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/v13.2/visualizations/simplified-exploration/logs/): Visualize log volumes to easily detect anomalies or significant changes over time, without needing to compose LogQL queries.
- [Grafana Traces Drilldown](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/v13.2/visualizations/simplified-exploration/traces/): Use Rate, Errors, and Duration (RED) metrics derived from traces to investigate and understand errors and latency issues within complex distributed systems.
- [Grafana Profiles Drilldown](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/v13.2/visualizations/simplified-exploration/profiles/): View and analyze high-level service performance, identify problem processes for optimization, and diagnose issues to determine root causes.
