# NetBird Support

### SupportBanner

- **Tone:** support
- **Badge:** Cloud & commercial
- **Description:** Reserved for (paying) Cloud customers and users, and self-hosted deployments on a commercial license. Use it for the Cloud dashboard, the managed control plane, billing, your subscription, or commercial self-hosted support. Open source self-hosted and general client questions belong in Community Support.
