# Report bugs and issues

NetBird offers two ways to get help, depending on what you are running. Pick the one that fits. Whichever you use, include a [debug bundle](https://docs.netbird.io/help/troubleshooting-client#debug-bundle), your NetBird version (`netbird version`), and clear steps to reproduce. For dashboard, login, or reverse-proxy issues, also [record a HAR file](https://docs.netbird.io/help/recording-a-har-file).

### Where to report

- **Id:** where-to-report
- **Items:** \[Community Support]\(/help/community-support) - Free, for everyone. The NetBird client, open source self-hosted deployments, and general questions, on Slack or GitHub Discussions.; \[NetBird Support]\(/help/netbird-support) - For (paying) Cloud customers and users, and commercial-license self-hosted deployments: the dashboard, control plane, billing, and subscriptions.
