# Community Support

### SupportBanner

- **Tone:** community
- **Badge:** Free · everyone
- **Description:** For everything outside the NetBird Cloud dashboard and managed infrastructure: the NetBird client, open source self-hosted deployments, and general questions. Open to all users.
- **Links:** \[Community Slack]\(#community-slack); \[GitHub Discussions]\(#git-hub-discussions)

Whichever channel you choose, include a [debug bundle](https://docs.netbird.io/help/troubleshooting-client#debug-bundle), your NetBird version (`netbird version`), and clear steps to reproduce. For dashboard, login, or reverse-proxy issues, also [record a HAR file](https://docs.netbird.io/help/recording-a-har-file).

## Community Slack

Best for quick questions or general configuration help. Join the NetBird community Slack to talk with other users and the team, and share debug output in a thread.

## GitHub Discussions

Best for bug reports, feature requests, and anything worth a searchable, written record. Open a thread in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/netbirdio/netbird/discussions) and include your version, environment, and a debug bundle.

For Cloud dashboard, billing, or commercial-license issues, use [NetBird Support](https://docs.netbird.io/help/netbird-support) instead.
