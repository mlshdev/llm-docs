# NetBird client on iOS

iOS-specific steps for the NetBird client. Most troubleshooting is cross-platform, so start from [Troubleshooting client issues](https://docs.netbird.io/help/troubleshooting-client).

On-device debugging on iOS is more limited than on desktop platforms, but the NetBird app can collect a debug bundle you can attach to a report.

> **Note**
>
> To collect a debug bundle on iOS, open the NetBird app and go to **Settings → Troubleshoot**. For status, connectivity, and DNS issues, also follow the cross-platform [Troubleshooting client issues](https://docs.netbird.io/help/troubleshooting-client) and [DNS Troubleshooting](https://docs.netbird.io/manage/dns/troubleshooting) guides. When you can reproduce a problem, report it with the debug bundle attached through [Community Support](https://docs.netbird.io/help/community-support), or through [NetBird Support](https://docs.netbird.io/help/netbird-support) if you are a paying customer.
