# EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType

**Framework:** RealityKit  
**Kind:** Enumeration  
**Availability:** iOS 18.0+ · iPadOS 18.0+ · Mac Catalyst 18.0+ · macOS 15.0+ · tvOS 26.0+ · visionOS 2.0+

Options available to determine the kinds of animation effects.

## Declaration

```swift
enum EmphasisMotionType
```

<a id="overview"></a>

## Overview

The [EmphasizeAction](../emphasizeaction.md) structure accepts this enumeration as an initializer argument.

## Topics

### Enumeration Cases

- [EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType.blink](emphasismotiontype/blink.md): Scales vertically, mimicking an eye blinking.
- [EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType.bounce](emphasismotiontype/bounce.md): Raises up and then drops, appearing to bounce on the ground.
- [EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType.flip](emphasismotiontype/flip.md): Raises up and rotates around a central axis before returning to its original position.
- [EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType.float](emphasismotiontype/float.md): Raises up and levitates for a moment before returning to its original position.
- [EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType.jiggle](emphasismotiontype/jiggle.md): Oscillates back and forth before returning to its original place.
- [EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType.pop](emphasismotiontype/pop.md): Grows like an inflating bubble before shrinking as if popped.
- [EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType.pulse](emphasismotiontype/pulse.md): Scales larger and smaller like a heartbeat.
- [EmphasizeAction.EmphasisMotionType.spin](emphasismotiontype/spin.md): Rotates around its central y-axis and returns to its original pose.
