## Topics

### Price increase status values

- [pending](status-swift.struct/pending.md): A value that indicates the App Store is waiting for customer consent for the price increase for the subscription renewal.
- [accepted](status-swift.struct/accepted.md): A value that indicates a customer accepted the new subscription price.
