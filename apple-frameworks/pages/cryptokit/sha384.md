## Overview

The [SHA384](sha384.md) hash implements the [HashFunction](hashfunction.md) protocol for the specific case of SHA-2 hashing with a 384-bit digest ([SHA384Digest](sha384digest.md)). Larger digests take more space but are more secure.

You can compute the digest by calling the static `hash(data:)` method once. Alternatively, if the data that you want to hash is too large to fit in memory, you can compute the digest iteratively by creating a new hash instance, calling the `update(data:)` method repeatedly with blocks of data, and then calling the [finalize()](sha384/finalize%28%29.md) method to get the result.
