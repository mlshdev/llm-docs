# WeatherKit Entitlement

**Interface languages:** Swift, Objective-C

**Framework:** Bundle Resources  
**Kind:** Property List Key  
**Availability:** iOS 16.0+ · iPadOS 16.0+ · Mac Catalyst 16.0+ · macOS 13.0+ · tvOS 16.0+ · watchOS 9.0+

A Boolean value that indicates whether the app may use WeatherKit.

## Details

`com.apple.developer.weatherkit`

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

To add this entitlement to your app, enable the WeatherKit capability in Xcode.
