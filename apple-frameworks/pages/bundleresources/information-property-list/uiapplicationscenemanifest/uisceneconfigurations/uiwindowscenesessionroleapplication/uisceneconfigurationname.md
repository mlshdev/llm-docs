# UISceneConfigurationName (Swift)

**Framework:** Bundle Resources  
**Kind:** Property List Key  
**Availability:** iOS 13.0+ · iPadOS 13.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.0+

The app-specific name you use to identify the scene.

## Details

`UISceneConfigurationName`

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

Assign all scene configurations a unique name to distinguish them in your app. Use this name in the [application(\_:configurationForConnecting:options:)](../../../../../uikit/uiapplicationdelegate/application%28__configurationforconnecting_options_%29.md) method of your app delegate to identify the requested configuration.
