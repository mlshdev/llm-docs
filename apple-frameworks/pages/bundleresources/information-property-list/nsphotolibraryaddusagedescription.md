# NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription

**Interface languages:** Swift, Objective-C

**Framework:** Bundle Resources  
**Kind:** Property List Key  
**Availability:** iOS 11.0+ · iPadOS 11.0+ · visionOS 1.0+

A message that tells people why the app is requesting add-only access to their photo library.

## Details

`NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

> **Important**

>  This key is required if your app uses APIs that have write access to the user’s photo library.
