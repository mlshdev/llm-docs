# NSHealthShareUsageDescription

**Interface languages:** Swift, Objective-C

**Framework:** Bundle Resources  
**Kind:** Property List Key  
**Availability:** iOS 8.0+ · iPadOS 8.0+ · visionOS 1.0+

A message that explains to people why the app requests permission to read samples from the HealthKit store.

## Details

`NSHealthShareUsageDescription`

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

> **Important**

>  This key is required if your app uses APIs that access the someone’s health data.
