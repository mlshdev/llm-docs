# NSRemindersUsageDescription

**Interface languages:** Swift, Objective-C

**Framework:** Bundle Resources  
**Kind:** Property List Key  
**Availability:** iOS 6.0+ (deprecated in 17.0) · iPadOS 6.0+ (deprecated in 17.0) · Mac Catalyst 13.0+ (deprecated in 17.0) · macOS 10.14+ (deprecated in 14.0) · watchOS 6.0+ (deprecated in 10.0)

A message that tells people why the app is requesting access to their reminders.

> NSRemindersUsageDescription has been deprecated; use [NSRemindersFullAccessUsageDescription](nsremindersfullaccessusagedescription.md) instead.

## Details

`NSRemindersUsageDescription`

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

> **Important**

>  This key is required if your app uses APIs that access the person’s reminders.
