**Availability:** iOS 26.0+ · iPadOS 26.0+ · Mac Catalyst 26.0+ · macOS 26.0+ · tvOS 26.0+ · visionOS 26.0+ · watchOS 26.0+

## Overview

> **Important**

> Note that this attribute is not for usage with CoreText APIs directly, but exposed to give other frameworks common vocabulary to express the line height concept as defined by CoreText in Swift. For more details, see [AttributeScopes.CoreTextAttributes](../coretextattributes.md).
