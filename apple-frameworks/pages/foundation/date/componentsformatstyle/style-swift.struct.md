### Type Properties

- [abbreviated](style-swift.struct/abbreviated.md): Shows the fields in the abbreviation. For example, “2 hr, 10 min”, “2小時10分鐘”
- [condensedAbbreviated](style-swift.struct/condensedabbreviated.md): Uses the abbreviated form but condensed if possible. For example, “2hr 10min”, “2小時10分鐘”
