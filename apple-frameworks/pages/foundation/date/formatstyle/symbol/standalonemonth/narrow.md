**Kind:** Type Property  
**Availability:** iOS 15.0+ · iPadOS 15.0+ · Mac Catalyst 15.0+ · macOS 12.0+ · tvOS 15.0+ · visionOS 1.0+ · watchOS 8.0+


## Discussion

This custom format style conveys the shortest representation of a month, like `O` (the letter) for October.
