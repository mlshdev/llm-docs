# Playlist.Kind

**Framework:** MusicKit  
**Kind:** Enumeration  
**Availability:** iOS 15.0+ · iPadOS 15.0+ · Mac Catalyst 15.0+ · macOS 12.0+ · tvOS 15.0+ · visionOS 1.0+ · watchOS 8.0+

The available kinds of playlists.

## Declaration

```swift
enum Kind
```

## Topics

### Enumeration Cases

- [Playlist.Kind.editorial](kind-swift.enum/editorial.md): Indicates that the playlist was created by an Apple Music curator.
- [Playlist.Kind.external](kind-swift.enum/external.md): Indicates that the playlist was created by an external curator.
- [Playlist.Kind.personalMix](kind-swift.enum/personalmix.md): Indicates that the playlist is a personalized playlist for an Apple Music user.
- [Playlist.Kind.replay](kind-swift.enum/replay.md): Indicates that the playlist is a personalized Replay playlist for an Apple Music user.
- [Playlist.Kind.userShared](kind-swift.enum/usershared.md): Indicates that the playlist was created and shared by an Apple Music user.
