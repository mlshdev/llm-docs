### Instance Properties

- [albumTitle](librarymusicvideosortproperties/albumtitle.md): The title of the album the music video appears on.
- [artistName](librarymusicvideosortproperties/artistname.md): The artist’s name.
- [duration](librarymusicvideosortproperties/duration.md): The duration of the music video.
- [lastPlayedDate](librarymusicvideosortproperties/lastplayeddate.md): The date when the user last played the music video on this device.
- [libraryAddedDate](librarymusicvideosortproperties/libraryaddeddate.md): The date when the user added the music video to the library.
- [playCount](librarymusicvideosortproperties/playcount.md): The number of times the user played the music video.
- [title](librarymusicvideosortproperties/title.md): The title of the music video.
- [trackNumber](librarymusicvideosortproperties/tracknumber.md): The music video’s number in the album’s track list.
