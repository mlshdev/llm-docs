# init(white_x:white_y:)

**Framework:** Accelerate  
**Kind:** Initializer  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst · macOS · tvOS · visionOS · watchOS

Creates a structure that represents a white point according to the CIE 1931 color space.
