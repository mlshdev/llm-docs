Perform two-pole two-zero recursive filtering on a vector.

## Topics

### Vector-to-Vector Recursive Filtering on Real Vectors

- [twoPoleTwoZeroFilter(\_:coefficients:)](vdsp/twopoletwozerofilter%28__coefficients_%29-8oaux.md): Returns the result of double-precision, two-pole, two-zero recursive filtering.
