# Direction Constants

**Interface language:** Objective-C

**Framework:** NetworkingDriverKit  
**Kind:** API Collection

Constants indicating package transmission direction.

## Topics

### Getting the Packet Directions

- [kIOUserNetworkPacketDirectionNone](kiousernetworkpacketdirectionnone.md): The packet is not moving.
- [kIOUserNetworkPacketDirectionRx](kiousernetworkpacketdirectionrx.md): The packet is moving from the device to the networking stack.
- [kIOUserNetworkPacketDirectionTx](kiousernetworkpacketdirectiontx.md): The packet is moving from the networking stack to the device.
