# PKPaymentButtonStyle.white (Swift)

**Framework:** PassKit (Apple Pay and Wallet)  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 8.3+ · iPadOS 8.3+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · macOS 11.0+ · visionOS 1.0+ · watchOS 3.0+

A white button with black lettering.

## Declaration

```swift
case white
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

Use the white button on backgrounds with colors that provide sufficient contrast. For white or light backgrounds, use either the white button with an outline or the black button.

## See Also

### Payment button styles

- [PKPaymentButtonStyle.whiteOutline](whiteoutline.md): A white button with black lettering and a black outline.
- [PKPaymentButtonStyle.black](black.md): A black button with white lettering.
