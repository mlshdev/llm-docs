# PKApplePayLaterAvailability.unavailableItemIneligible (Swift)

**Framework:** PassKit (Apple Pay and Wallet)  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 17.0+ · iPadOS 17.0+ · Mac Catalyst 17.0+ · macOS 14.0+ · visionOS 1.0+ · watchOS 10.0+

Apple Pay Later is unavailable because one or more ineligible or prohibited items are in the shopping cart, such as gift cards.

> Apple Pay Later is deprecated.
