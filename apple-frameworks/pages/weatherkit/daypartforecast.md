- [precipitation](daypartforecast/precipitation.md): The description of precipitation for the day part.
- [precipitationAmountByType](daypartforecast/precipitationamountbytype.md): A breakdown of all precipitation forecasted for the day.
- [precipitationChance](daypartforecast/precipitationchance.md): The probability of precipitation for the day part.
