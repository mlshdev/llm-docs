**Framework:** Video Toolbox  
**Kind:** Initializer  
**Availability:** iOS 6.0+ · iPadOS 6.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.0+ · macOS 10.8+ · tvOS 10.2+ · visionOS 1.0+
