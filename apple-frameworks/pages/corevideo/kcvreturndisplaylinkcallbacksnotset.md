# kCVReturnDisplayLinkCallbacksNotSet (Swift)

**Framework:** Core Video  
**Kind:** Global Variable  
**Availability:** iOS 4.0+ · iPadOS 4.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.0+ · macOS 10.4+ · tvOS 9.0+ · visionOS 1.0+ · watchOS 4.0+
