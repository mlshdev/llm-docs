**Framework:** WidgetKit  
**Kind:** Type Property  
**Availability:** iOS 18.0+ · iPadOS 18.0+ · Mac Catalyst 18.0+ · macOS 15.0+ · visionOS 26.0+ · watchOS 11.0+
