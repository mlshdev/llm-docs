# CLKComplicationFamily.graphicBezel (Swift)

**Framework:** ClockKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** watchOS 5.0+

A circular area with optional curved text that ClockKit displays along the bezel of the Infograph watch face.

## Declaration

```swift
case graphicBezel
```
