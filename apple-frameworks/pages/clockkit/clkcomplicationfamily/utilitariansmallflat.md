# CLKComplicationFamily.utilitarianSmallFlat (Swift)

**Framework:** ClockKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** watchOS 3.0+

A small rectangular area that ClockKit Displays on the Photos, Motion, and Timelapse watch faces.

## Declaration

```swift
case utilitarianSmallFlat
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

The following figure shows the size and location of the utilitarian small flat complication.

![Diagram showing the size and position of a utilitarian small flat complication.](https://developer.apple.com/images/com.apple.clockkit/media-2880717@2x.png)

The utilitarian small flat family can display only the [CLKComplicationTemplateUtilitarianSmallFlat](../clkcomplicationtemplateutilitariansmallflat.md) template.
