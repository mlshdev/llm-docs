# HMCharacteristicTypeLabelNamespace (Swift)

**Framework:** HomeKit  
**Kind:** Global Variable  
**Availability:** iOS 10.3+ · iPadOS 10.3+ · Mac Catalyst 10.3+ · tvOS 10.2+ · visionOS 1.0+ · watchOS 3.2+
