# Characteristic Units

**Interface languages:** Swift, Objective-C

**Framework:** HomeKit  
**Kind:** API Collection

Descriptions of the units of a characteristic.

<a id="overview"></a>

## Overview

Expect to find one of these values in the [units](hmcharacteristicmetadata/units.md) property of a characteristic’s metadata.

## Topics

### Parts Per Total

- [HMCharacteristicMetadataUnitsPercentage](hmcharacteristicmetadataunitspercentage.md): The unit of the characteristic is a percentage.
- [HMCharacteristicMetadataUnitsPartsPerMillion](hmcharacteristicmetadataunitspartspermillion.md): The unit of the characters is parts per million.

### Temperature

- [HMCharacteristicMetadataUnitsCelsius](hmcharacteristicmetadataunitscelsius.md): The unit of the characteristic is celsius.
- [HMCharacteristicMetadataUnitsFahrenheit](hmcharacteristicmetadataunitsfahrenheit.md): The unit of the characteristic is fahrenheit.

### Time

- [HMCharacteristicMetadataUnitsSeconds](hmcharacteristicmetadataunitsseconds.md): The unit of the characteristic is seconds.

### Light

- [HMCharacteristicMetadataUnitsLux](hmcharacteristicmetadataunitslux.md): The unit of the characteristic is lux (that is, illuminance).

### Mass

- [HMCharacteristicMetadataUnitsMicrogramsPerCubicMeter](hmcharacteristicmetadataunitsmicrogramspercubicmeter.md): The unit of the characteristic is micrograms per cubic meter.

### Rotation

- [HMCharacteristicMetadataUnitsArcDegree](hmcharacteristicmetadataunitsarcdegree.md): The unit of the characteristic is the degrees of an arc.
