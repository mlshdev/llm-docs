# UIPress.PressType.pageDown (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** tvOS 14.3+

A constant that represents the page down button.

## Declaration

```swift
case pageDown
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

Add gesture recognizers for [UIPress.PressType.pageDown](pagedown.md) and [UIPress.PressType.pageUp](pageup.md) to navigate content in your app.

In an electronic program guide, select the item below the current one in response to [UIPress.PressType.downArrow](downarrow.md), and display the next screen of items in response to [UIPress.PressType.pageDown](pagedown.md). While your content plays, change the channel when your app receives [UIPress.PressType.pageUp](pageup.md) or [UIPress.PressType.pageDown](pagedown.md).

For more details on browsing multichannel content, see [Providing Channel Navigation](../../../tvservices/providing-channel-navigation.md).
