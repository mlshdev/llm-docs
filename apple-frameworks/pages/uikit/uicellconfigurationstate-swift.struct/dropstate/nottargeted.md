# UICellConfigurationState.DropState.notTargeted

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 14.0+ · iPadOS 14.0+ · Mac Catalyst 14.0+ · tvOS 14.0+ · visionOS
