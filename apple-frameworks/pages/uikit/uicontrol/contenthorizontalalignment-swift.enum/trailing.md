# UIControl.ContentHorizontalAlignment.trailing (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 11.0+ · iPadOS 11.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · tvOS 11.0+ · visionOS 1.0+

Aligns the content horizontally from the trailing edge of the control.

## Declaration

```swift
case trailing
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

For left-to-right languages, the trailing edge is the right edge. For right-to-left languages, the trailing edge is the left edge.

## See Also

### Constants

- [UIControl.ContentHorizontalAlignment.center](center.md): Aligns the content horizontally in the center of the control.
- [UIControl.ContentHorizontalAlignment.left](left.md): Aligns the content horizontally from the left of the control (the default).
- [UIControl.ContentHorizontalAlignment.right](right.md): Aligns the content horizontally from the right of the control.
- [UIControl.ContentHorizontalAlignment.fill](fill.md): Aligns the content horizontally to fill the content rectangles; text may wrap and images may be stretched.
- [UIControl.ContentHorizontalAlignment.leading](leading.md): Aligns the content horizontally from the leading edge of the control.
