# UIScreen.ReferenceDisplayModeStatus.limited (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 16.0+ · iPadOS 16.0+ · Mac Catalyst 16.0+ · tvOS 16.0+

A status that indicates the screen’s in a limited reference display mode.

## Declaration

```swift
case limited
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

The screen may be unable to provide an accurate reference display mode due to thermal or power constraints.
