# UIBarPosition.bottom (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 7.0+ · iPadOS 7.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · tvOS · visionOS 1.0+

Specifies that the bar is at the bottom of its containing view.

## Declaration

```swift
case bottom
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

The system uses this as a hint to draw directional decoration accordingly. For example, any shadow would be drawn above the bar.

## See Also

### Constants

- [UIBarPosition.any](any.md): Specifies that the position is unspecified.
- [UIBarPosition.top](top.md): Specifies that the bar is at the top of its containing view.
- [UIBarPosition.topAttached](topattached.md): Specifies that the bar is at the top of the screen, as well as its containing view.
