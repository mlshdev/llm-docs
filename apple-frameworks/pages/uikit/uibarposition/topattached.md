# UIBarPosition.topAttached (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 7.0+ · iPadOS 7.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · tvOS · visionOS 1.0+

Specifies that the bar is at the top of the screen, as well as its containing view.

## Declaration

```swift
case topAttached
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

Bars with this position draw their background extended upwards, allowing their background content to show through the status bar.

## See Also

### Constants

- [UIBarPosition.any](any.md): Specifies that the position is unspecified.
- [UIBarPosition.bottom](bottom.md): Specifies that the bar is at the bottom of its containing view.
- [UIBarPosition.top](top.md): Specifies that the bar is at the top of its containing view.
