# UITextDropEditability.temporary (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 11.0+ · iPadOS 11.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · visionOS 1.0+

A text-drop editability specifier indicating that a noneditable text view does accept drops but reverts to its noneditable status immediately afterward.

## Declaration

```swift
case temporary
```

## See Also

### Editability styles

- [UITextDropEditability.no](no.md): A text-drop editability specifier indicating that a noneditable text view does not accept drops.
- [UITextDropEditability.yes](yes.md): A text-drop editability specifier indicating that a noneditable text view does accept drops, and that the dropped text remains editable after the drop is finished.
