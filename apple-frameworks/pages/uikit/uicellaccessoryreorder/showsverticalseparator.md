# showsVerticalSeparator

**Interface language:** Objective-C

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Instance Property  
**Availability:** iOS 14.0+ · iPadOS 14.0+ · Mac Catalyst 14.0+ · tvOS 14.0+ · visionOS 1.0+
