# tintedButtonConfiguration

**Interface language:** Objective-C

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Type Method  
**Availability:** iOS 15.0+ · iPadOS 15.0+ · Mac Catalyst 15.0+ · tvOS 15.0+ · visionOS 1.0+
