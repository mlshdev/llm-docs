# UITab.Placement.automatic (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 18.0+ · iPadOS 18.0+ · Mac Catalyst 18.0+ · tvOS 18.0+ · visionOS 2.0+

The system adds only top-level items to the tab bar, but people can add, remove, or move this item.

## Declaration

```swift
case automatic
```

## See Also

### Placement values

- [UITab.Placement.default](default.md): The item appears in the tab bar, but people can move or remove it.
- [UITab.Placement.fixed](fixed.md): The item appears in the tab bar’s leading edge, and people can’t move or remove it.
- [UITab.Placement.movable](movable.md): The item appears in the tab bar, people can move it but can’t remove it.
- [UITab.Placement.optional](optional.md): The item doesn’t appear in the tab bar, but people can add it and move it.
- [UITab.Placement.pinned](pinned.md): The item appears as a pinned tab, on the trailing edge of the tab bar.
- [UITab.Placement.sidebarOnly](sidebaronly.md): The item only appears in the sidebar.
