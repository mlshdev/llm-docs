# UILineBreakModeCharacterWrap

**Interface language:** Objective-C

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Enumeration Case  
**Availability:** iOS 2.0+ (deprecated in 6.0) · iPadOS 2.0+ (deprecated in 6.0) · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ (deprecated in 13.1)
