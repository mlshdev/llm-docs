# UIToolbarPosition

**Interface language:** Objective-C

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Macro  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst · tvOS · visionOS · watchOS

Constants to identify the position of a toolbar for appearance customization.

## Declaration

```objectivec
#define UIToolbarPosition
```

<a id="overview"></a>

## Overview

In iOS 7 and later, these constants are defined in terms of [UIBarPosition](uibarposition.md), which also introduces a new possible position: [UIBarPositionTopAttached](uibarposition/topattached.md).

## Topics

### Positions

- [UIToolbarPositionAny](uitoolbarpositionany.md): Deprecated. Indicates the toolbar may be in any position.
- [UIToolbarPositionBottom](uitoolbarpositionbottom.md): Indicates the toolbar is at the top of its containing view.
- [UIToolbarPositionTop](uitoolbarpositiontop.md): Indicates the toolbar is at the bottom of its containing view.
