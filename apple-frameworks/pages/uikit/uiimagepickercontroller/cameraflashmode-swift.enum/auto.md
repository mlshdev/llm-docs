# UIImagePickerController.CameraFlashMode.auto (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst

Specifies that the device should consider ambient light conditions to automatically determine whether or not to use flash illumination.

## Declaration

```swift
case auto
```

### Constants
