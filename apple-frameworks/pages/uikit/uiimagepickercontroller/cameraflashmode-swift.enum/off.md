# UIImagePickerController.CameraFlashMode.off (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst

Specifies that flash illumination is always off, no matter what the ambient light conditions are.
