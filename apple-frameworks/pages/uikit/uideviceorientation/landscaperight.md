# UIDeviceOrientation.landscapeRight (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst · visionOS

The device is in landscape mode, with the device held upright and the front-facing camera on the right side.

## Declaration

```swift
case landscapeRight
```
