# UIDeviceOrientation.portrait (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst · visionOS

The device is in portrait mode, with the device held upright and the front-facing camera at the top.

## Declaration

```swift
case portrait
```

### Device orientations

- [UIDeviceOrientation.unknown](unknown.md): The orientation of the device can’t be determined.
- [UIDeviceOrientation.portraitUpsideDown](portraitupsidedown.md): The device is in portrait mode but upside down, with the device held upright and the front-facing camera at the bottom.
