# trailingPadding (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Instance Property  
**Availability:** iOS 15.0+ · iPadOS 15.0+ · Mac Catalyst 15.0+ · tvOS 15.0+ · visionOS 1.0+

The amount of margin space reserved during paragraph layout between the end of the lines in the paragraph and the trailing edge of the text layout fragment.

## Declaration

```swift
var trailingPadding: CGFloat { get }
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

This is with respect to the primary writing direction of the paragraph.

## See Also

### Defining margins and padding

- [bottomMargin](bottommargin.md): The amount of space reserved during paragraph layout between the bottom of the last line in the paragraph and the bottom of the text layout fragment.
- [leadingPadding](leadingpadding.md): The amount of margin space reserved during paragraph layout between the leading edge of the text layout fragment and the start of the lines in the paragraph.
- [topMargin](topmargin.md): The amount of space reserved during paragraph layout between the top of the text layout fragment and the top of the first line in the paragraph.
