# UITitlebarSeparatorStyle (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Enumeration  
**Availability:** Mac Catalyst 14.0+

Styles that determine the type of separator displayed between the title bar and content of a window.

## Declaration

```swift
enum UITitlebarSeparatorStyle
```

## Topics

### Styles

- [UITitlebarSeparatorStyle.automatic](uititlebarseparatorstyle/automatic.md): A style indicating that the system determines the type of separator.

- [UITitlebarSeparatorStyle.none](uititlebarseparatorstyle/none.md): A style indicating that there’s no title bar separator.
- [UITitlebarSeparatorStyle.shadow](uititlebarseparatorstyle/shadow.md): A style indicating that the title bar separator is a shadow.
