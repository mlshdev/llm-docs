# UIInterfaceOrientation.portraitUpsideDown (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst · visionOS

The device is in portrait mode but is upside down, with the device upright and the Home button at the top.

## Declaration

```swift
case portraitUpsideDown
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

[UIViewController](../uiviewcontroller.md) doesn’t support this case on devices without a Home button.

## See Also

### Orientations

- [UIInterfaceOrientation.unknown](unknown.md): The orientation of the device is unknown.
- [UIInterfaceOrientation.portrait](portrait.md): The device is in portrait mode, with the device upright and the Home button on the bottom.
- [UIInterfaceOrientation.landscapeLeft](landscapeleft.md): The device is in landscape mode, with the device upright and the Home button on the left.
- [UIInterfaceOrientation.landscapeRight](landscaperight.md): The device is in landscape mode, with the device upright and the Home button on the right.
