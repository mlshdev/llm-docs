# UIView.AnimationCurve.easeOut (Swift)

**Framework:** UIKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst · tvOS · visionOS

An ease-out curve causes the animation to begin quickly, and then slow down as it completes.

## Declaration

```swift
case easeOut
```

## See Also

### Constants

- [UIView.AnimationCurve.easeInOut](easeinout.md): An ease-in ease-out curve causes the animation to begin slowly, accelerate through the middle of its duration, and then slow again before completing. This is the default curve for most animations.
- [UIView.AnimationCurve.easeIn](easein.md): An ease-in curve causes the animation to begin slowly, and then speed up as it progresses.
- [UIView.AnimationCurve.linear](linear.md): A linear animation curve causes an animation to occur evenly over its duration.
