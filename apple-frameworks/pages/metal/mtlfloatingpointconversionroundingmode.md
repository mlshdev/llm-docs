## Declaration

enum MTLFloatingPointConversionRoundingMode

<a id="overview"></a>

## Overview

The rounding mode for narrowing floating-point conversions.

Round-to-nearest-even (default). Standard IEEE 754 rounding mode.

Round-toward-zero.
