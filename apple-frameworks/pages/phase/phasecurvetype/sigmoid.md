# PHASECurveType.sigmoid (Swift)

**Framework:** PHASE  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS · iPadOS · Mac Catalyst · macOS · tvOS · visionOS

A sigmoid curve.

## Declaration

```swift
case sigmoid
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

Also known as an *s-curve*, the sigmoid curve’s path movement is slow at either end and quick in the middle.
