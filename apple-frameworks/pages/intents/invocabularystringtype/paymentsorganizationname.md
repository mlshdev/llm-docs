# INVocabularyStringType.paymentsOrganizationName (Swift)

**Framework:** Intents  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 10.3+ · iPadOS 10.3+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · tvOS 10.2+ · visionOS 1.0+ · watchOS 6.0+

The name of the bank or company that holds the user’s account.

## Declaration

```swift
case paymentsOrganizationName
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

Siri puts the organization name in the `organizationName` property of the [INPaymentAccount](../inpaymentaccount.md) and [INBillPayee](../inbillpayee.md) objects.
