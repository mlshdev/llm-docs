**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 13.0+ · iPadOS 13.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · tvOS 14.0+ · visionOS 1.0+ · watchOS 6.0+

case unknown

## See Also

### Constants
