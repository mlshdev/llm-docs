**Framework:** PDFKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 11.0+ · iPadOS 11.0+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · macOS 10.4+ · tvOS 11.0+ · visionOS 1.0+

## Declaration

```swift
case twoUp
```

## Discussion
