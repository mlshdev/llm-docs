# MPMediaLibraryAuthorizationStatus.authorized (Swift)

**Framework:** Media Player  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 9.3+ · iPadOS 9.3+ · Mac Catalyst 13.1+ · visionOS 1.0+

Your app may access items in the user’s media library.

## Declaration

```swift
case authorized
```

## See Also

### Authorization statuses

- [MPMediaLibraryAuthorizationStatus.notDetermined](notdetermined.md): The user hasn’t determined whether to authorize the use of their media library.
- [MPMediaLibraryAuthorizationStatus.denied](denied.md): The app may not access the items in the user’s media library.
- [MPMediaLibraryAuthorizationStatus.restricted](restricted.md): The app may access some of the content in the user’s media library.
