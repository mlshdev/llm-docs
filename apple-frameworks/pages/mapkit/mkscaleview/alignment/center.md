# MKScaleView.Alignment.center (Swift)

**Framework:** MapKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** iOS 26.0+ · iPadOS 26.0+ · Mac Catalyst 26.0+ · tvOS 26.0+ · visionOS 26.0+

Scale measurements appear horizontally centered within the view.

## Declaration

```swift
case center
```

<a id="Discussion"></a>

## Discussion

The framework depicts the scale view distance using a legend that grows and shrinks around its horizontal center point.

## See Also

### Alignment options

- [MKScaleView.Alignment.leading](leading.md): Scale measurements begin at the leading edge of the view.
- [MKScaleView.Alignment.trailing](trailing.md): Scale measurements begin at the trailing edge of the view.
