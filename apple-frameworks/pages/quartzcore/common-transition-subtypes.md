# Common Transition Subtypes (Swift)

**Framework:** Core Animation  
**Kind:** API Collection

These constants specify the direction of motion-based transitions. They are used with the [subtype](catransition/subtype.md) property.

## Topics

### Constants

- [fromRight](catransitionsubtype/fromright.md): The transition begins at the right side of the layer.
- [fromLeft](catransitionsubtype/fromleft.md): The transition begins at the left side of the layer.
- [fromTop](catransitionsubtype/fromtop.md): The transition begins at the top of the layer.
- [fromBottom](catransitionsubtype/frombottom.md): The transition begins at the bottom of the layer.

## See Also

### Constants

- [Common Transition Types](common-transition-types.md): These constants specify the transition types that can be used with the [type](catransition/type.md) property.
