# Stop Speech Locations

Locations that indicate where speech should be paused or stopped.

## Overview

See the functions [PauseSpeechAt(\_:\_:)](../1461174-pausespeechat.md) and [StopSpeechAt(\_:\_:)](../1459780-stopspeechat.md) for more information.

### Constants

- [kImmediate](../kimmediate.md): Speech should be paused or stopped immediately.
- [kEndOfWord](../kendofword.md): Speech should be paused or stopped at the endof the word.
- [kEndOfSentence](../kendofsentence.md): Speech should be paused or stopped at the end of the sentence.
