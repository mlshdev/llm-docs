# NSCollectionLayoutSectionOrthogonalScrollingBehavior.continuousGroupLeadingBoundary (Swift)

**Framework:** AppKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** macOS 10.15+

The section allows users to scroll its content orthogonally, coming to a natural stop at the leading boundary of the visible group.

## Declaration

```swift
case continuousGroupLeadingBoundary
```
