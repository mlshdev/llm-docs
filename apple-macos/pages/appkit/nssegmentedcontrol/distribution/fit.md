# NSSegmentedControl.Distribution.fit (Swift)

**Framework:** AppKit  
**Kind:** Case  
**Availability:** macOS 10.13+

Dynamically sized segments will be sized to fit their contents, any remaining space will be left blank. This style is equivalent to the way segments were distributed on older systems.

## Declaration

```swift
case fit
```

## See Also

### Distribution Options

- [NSSegmentedControl.Distribution.fill](fill.md): Dynamically sized segments will be sized to fill the available space, with extra space being distributed equally among them. Default value.
- [NSSegmentedControl.Distribution.fillEqually](fillequally.md): Dynamically sized segments will be sized to fill the available space, and kept the same size as each other.
- [NSSegmentedControl.Distribution.fillProportionally](fillproportionally.md): Dynamically sized segments will be sized to fill the available space, and kept proportional to their fitting size.
