# Quick food ordering

**Interface languages:** Swift, Objective-C

**Framework:** CarPlay

Build an app that helps someone order food for pickup.

<a id="overview"></a>

## Overview

A quick food-ordering app helps people order food from their vehicle. Create this type of app if you have a Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) app and support mobile food orders and pickup and delivery scheduling. Build your app’s interface to support streamlined ordering, rather than require people to navigate your restaurant’s menu.

This type of app supports the following CarPlay templates:

- [CPInformationTemplate](cpinformationtemplate.md)
- [CPPointOfInterestTemplate](cppointofinteresttemplate.md)
- [CPTabBarTemplate](cptabbartemplate.md)
- [CPListTemplate](cplisttemplate.md)
- [CPGridTemplate](cpgridtemplate.md)
- [CPVoiceControlTemplate](cpvoicecontroltemplate.md)
- [CPActionSheetTemplate](cpactionsheettemplate.md)
- [CPAlertTemplate](cpalerttemplate.md)
