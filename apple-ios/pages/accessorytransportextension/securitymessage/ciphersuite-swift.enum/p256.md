## Discussion

Use this cipher suite as a fallback for Bluetooth only. The [AccessoryTransport.internet](../../accessorytransport/internet.md) or [AccessoryTransport.localNetwork](../../accessorytransport/localnetwork.md) transport types require [SecurityMessage.CipherSuite.xWing](xwing.md) instead.
