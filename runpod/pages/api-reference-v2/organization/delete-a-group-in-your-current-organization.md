# Delete A Group In Your Current Organization

Delete a group and its group memberships without removing anyone from the organization.

`DELETE /v2/organization/groups/{id}`

**Delete a group in your current organization**

Permanently deletes the group and its group memberships. Organization members are not removed. Returns 404 when the group does not exist in your current organization, including when it has already been deleted.

**Authentication:** `bearerAuth`

**Parameters**

- `id` (path; required; string; minimum length: `1`): Group ID.
